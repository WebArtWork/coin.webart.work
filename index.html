02 / How it works

Ready for a New Game?
Your Coins Come With You.

01Play

Play Game A and earn wCoin through its supported rewards.

02Buy

Buy a skin you want to use.

03List

When you move on, list the eligible skin on the marketplace.

04Sell

When another player buys it, receive wCoin after any applicable fees.

05Play again

Use those coins to buy an item in Game B.

Items stay connected to their games. The coins from a completed sale can be spent in another participating game.

Sales depend on buyer demand. Prices and fees affect the coins you receive. Not every item is guaranteed to sell.

18–20 / Built for use, not speculation

A Currency You're Meant to Use.

wCoin is designed around gameplay and the item marketplace, not around price speculation. Trading eligible items with other players is part of the intended experience.

Marketplace trading, by design

Buying and selling eligible items between players is a planned, supported part of wCoin.

External exchange and cash-out: undecided

Listing wCoin on external exchanges, cashing it out, or setting its external price are separate product decisions not covered here.
